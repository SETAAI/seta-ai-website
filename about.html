About

Who we are

SETA AI exists because of a simple observation from twenty years inside enterprise SAP landscapes: the hardest indirect-tax problems were never solved by the ERP — they were solved by custom code bolted onto it. That era is ending. Clean core evicts the custom logic; someone has to give it a better home.

We build that home on SAP BTP: a deterministic tax engine whose content is drafted by AI with legal citations, reviewed and approved by tax professionals, and governed like the system of record it becomes. Every answer explainable. Every change documented. Every country faster than the last.

Built by SAP tax people, for SAP tax people

SETA AI was founded by Vijay Gummadi, a principal SAP indirect-tax solution architect with more than twenty years designing and running tax determination for global enterprises — including some of the most complex custom SAP tax landscapes in the Fortune 500. Having delivered SAP tax determination and tax-engine integrations for close to 150 countries, he knows what it takes to keep such a landscape right, year after year — and designed SETA AI from that experience. We know exactly what the old way costs, because we built it, ran it, and answered for it in audits. SETA AI is the way out.
